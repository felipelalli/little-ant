# 1. Purpose and authority

This document set materializes the decisions made during the Little Ant 1.0
discovery session before the Allium specification, README, operator skill, CLI
contract, or implementation is changed.

It has four jobs:

1. preserve confirmed decisions;
2. make later corrections override earlier proposals explicitly;
3. retain unresolved questions without silently choosing answers;
4. provide a durable hand-off for the remaining discovery, old-Brick review,
   specification work, and v0-to-1.0 migration plan.

Until the design is transferred into the Allium specification, this collection
is the canonical record of the 1.0 discussion. If an earlier session summary
conflicts with this collection, **Corrections and superseded decisions** wins.
The Allium specification becomes authoritative again only after the
documentation phase is deliberately completed and reviewed.

The regression-recovery review that began on 2026-07-28 is recorded
incrementally in **V1 recovery ledger**. A confirmed recovery entry is the
latest decision and overrides conflicting wording in an earlier subject
chapter, README, Allium declaration, generated test, v0 operator skill, or
failed implementation. The override remains explicit in the ledger until it
has been propagated and reviewed across the relevant artifacts.

The words **must**, **must not**, **should**, and **may** distinguish confirmed
requirements from recommendations. Text under **Open questions** is
deliberately non-normative.
